Login background video
======================
Put the finished video here with exactly these names:

  login-loop.mp4   (required)  H.264, 1920x1080 or 1280x720, 6-10 s, no sound, under 5 MB
  login-loop.webm  (optional)  same clip as VP9/WebM, usually smaller
  login-loop.jpg   (optional)  one still frame, shown while the video loads

The login page picks it up automatically. Without these files the animated
drawing of the car is shown instead, so nothing breaks.
